# Seri_Malaya_Dessert_Cafe

Seri Malaya Dessert Cafe is a store that offers various types of dessert including local and non local desserts. The desserts are divided into four primary types, ‘cake’, ‘pastry’, ‘traditional’ or ‘others’. The dessert which does not fall into the first three types belongs to the ‘other’ type. This cafe does not have a system as it was a small business. This means the employees of this cafe do everything manually. Nevertheless, they can still manage as the amount the customer they receive is small.

However, the success and popularity it suddenly gained brought us some challenges. The cafe, which is now overflowing with customers, burdens the employee as they write their orders on a piece of paper.  Other employees are having a hard time understanding the employee handwriting. This causes unwanted waste of time. There also are some occurrences where the order sequence of customers is mixed up causing the customer's anger. It is also hard to find a customer order when the paper we wrote them on is lost. Employees are also taking a long time to calculate the total payment for each customer and there are some cases where their calculations are wrong. 

To overcome this issue, we decided to develop a system in Java where it can run the tasks while ensuring the cafe runs efficiently and smoothly. This customer order will be stored in the system, preventing the loss of their orders. The search of a specific customer’s order can also be done easily. We also can update the price of our products through the system. Most importantly, the total payment for each customer is now calculated automatically in the system. 

However we are not sure which data structure to use in our system, linked list or queue. Both have their advantages and disadvantages. To decide which one is more suitable to be implemented in our cafe system, we develop the prototype using linked list and queue separately. This will help us to find which one is the best to be applied in our case study.
